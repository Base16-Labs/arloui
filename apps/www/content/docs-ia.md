# ArloUI documentation information architecture

## Primitives

- `/docs/primitives/tokens` — Theme source and raw values
- `/docs/primitives/type` — Font configuration, 16-token scale, weights, and specimens
- `/docs/primitives/color` — Palettes and semantic colors
- `/docs/primitives/spacing` — Spacing, sizing, and touch targets
- `/docs/primitives/motion` — Durations, easing, and springs
- `/docs/primitives/effects` — Shadows, focus rings, blurs, and glass materials
- `/docs/primitives/icons` — Static and animated iconography
