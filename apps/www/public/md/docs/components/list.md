# List

> A compound stacked-row layout: List is the container, List.Row is the item. Rows take a leading icon or media, a title over an optional subtitle, and a trailing value alongside an optional icon. It draws no surface of its own — set separated to space rows onto their own surfaces, or keep them contiguous with an inset, balanced, edge, or no hairline.
